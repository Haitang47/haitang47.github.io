About me

I am an MSc student in Computer Control & Automation at Nanyang Technological University. My research interests include aerial manipulation, robot learning, and motion planning.

I am interested in how robots perceive and interact with the physical world. My work connects mechanical design with perception and control, with experience in robot arm development, ROS integration, and robotics competitions.
